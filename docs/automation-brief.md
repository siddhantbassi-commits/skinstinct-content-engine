# Automation Brief — Case 1 / Meera

## Why

**Pain:** 60 voice notes captured over 8 months and never used, 40 abandoned Google
Drive drafts, 11 weeks without a LinkedIn post. The gap between capture and publish is
documented and ongoing, and it has a measurable commercial cost — her last post drove
340 profile visits in 48 hours and converted 3 wholesale enquiries.

**User:** Meera Pillai, founder of Skinstinct.

**Outcome:** Three publishable LinkedIn posts a week, under 15 minutes of Meera's
active time per week.

## Journey Today

1. Meera drops a note into her personal Telegram channel.
2. The note sits there — nothing processes it.
3. Occasionally she opens Google Drive, finds an old note or draft, starts writing.
4. She writes two lines, decides it isn't good enough, closes the doc.
5. The draft joins 40 others in the abandoned folder.
6. Nothing gets published.

## Journey With the Tool

1. Meera drops the same note into the same Telegram channel — no new habit required.
2. The bot scores it 0–10 for whether it has a real point. Below 6, she gets a
   one-line rejection and nothing else happens — no time spent on it.
3. If it passes, the pipeline finds a relevant, current news item (only if genuinely
   relevant) and drafts a full LinkedIn post in her voice, built from the note.
4. The draft arrives back in the same Telegram chat, with the news source flagged for
   her to verify if one was used.
5. She replies APPROVE or REJECT. Either way, nothing reaches LinkedIn automatically —
   she copies the approved draft and publishes it herself.
